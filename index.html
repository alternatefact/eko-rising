Eko Rising

Start with a hoe, a wife and a patch of land by the lagoon. Build Lagos — and decide how the history of a nation goes.

New game

The map of Nigeria

Chapter one is Lagos. New states open in future updates.

How to play
  • Build from the bar at the bottom. Woodcutters, farms, hunters and fishers each need one worker.
  • Strangers come when your people are fed and happy. They wait at the campfire (or the town square once you have a palace) until you build them a home — then they move in and go to work.
  • Workers fill the most important jobs first. Set a building's priority to High and someone will leave lower-priority work for it.
  • Explore with scouts. Other peoples may be friendly — or strong enough to wipe out your soldiers. Befriend them, make them pay tribute, or conquer them and take their wealth.
  • History arrives when you are ready for it: Benin, the Portuguese, the French, the British. You decide what happens.
  • Controls: drag to move the map, pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap to select. With soldiers selected, tap the ground to move or an enemy to attack. On a computer: right-click to command, Shift-drag to box-select, WASD to scroll.
