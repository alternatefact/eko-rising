Build your Nigeria

Start with a hoe, a wife and a patch of land by the lagoon. Build Lagos — and decide how the history of a nation goes.

New game

The map of Nigeria

Chapter one is Èkó, in Lagos. Break the host of Benin there to open Ogun State; more states open in future updates.

How to play
  • The guide at the top left walks you through the first days, one step at a time. Nothing moves on until you have done what it asks. When it asks for a building, one is enough: the arrow moves to the building site and waits while your people put it up. Untick Guided start to build freely from the first day.
  • Wood first: tap the founder, then a tree, and he fells it and carries the planks home. Wood sheds, hunters and farms each take their own hands. Trees are walls: clear them to open ground and paths.
  • Newcomers come when your people are fed and happy and there are beds. They sit at the campfire until there is work. Each new house brings two.
  • Repairs: the house you find is a wreck: tap the founder, then the house, then 🔨 Repair. Anything damaged in war shows a 🔨: tap it and press Repair, if you have the materials.
  • Building: nothing builds itself. Whoever sits by the fire (and the founder, when he is free) carries the materials over and puts each building up, stage by stage. Tap a worker, then a building site, to send them to help.
  • Moving people: tap a worker, then the workplace you want them at. They change into work clothes, then walk there. Tap a woodcutter, then a tree, to choose where he cuts.
  • Explore with scouts. Other peoples may be friendly — or strong enough to wipe out your soldiers. Befriend them, make them pay tribute, or conquer them and take their wealth.
  • History arrives when you are ready for it: Benin, the Portuguese, the French, the British. You decide what happens. Defy Benin, or buy time with gifts, and the Oba's host of fifty gathers at the far end of the land: break it to win Lagos and open Ogun State.
  • Ogun: you start again from scratch, but everything Lagos learned comes with you: what it could build you can build from the first day, and the ages it reached come quicker. Ogun has its own enemies: the Alaafin's horsemen, Ìjẹ̀bú gunmen, and the host of Dahomey with its women soldiers, the Agojie.
  • Gathering point: 🚩 in the army panel or a Guard House plants a flag where your army gathers. New soldiers walk to it, and Go to the flag calls everyone there.
  • Full stores: a warning at the top tells you when there is no room left. Workers wait at their workplaces with their loads until you build another Storage Floor, granary or storehouse.
  • Soldiers: choose Stand your ground or Fight any close by. Fighting soldiers go for enemies, tents and huts that come near. On a phone, press ⬚ Select and drag a box round the soldiers you want, or tap them one by one.
  • Seeing behind things: ⟲ ⟳ beside the map turn the view a quarter turn (Q and E on a keyboard), 🌳 makes the trees see-through (T), and ⌂ brings you back to the village (H). Trees in front of whatever you have selected fade by themselves. While you build a fence, every gap still open is marked in red, even behind the trees.
  • Tell us what you think: 💬 Send feedback, here or in the ☰ menu, goes straight to the alternatefact Studios team. We read every message.
  • Saving: 💾 at the top saves in one of three slots; the game also saves itself every minute. The ☰ menu has Save game and Load game. Save to a file to keep a game safe or carry on on another device.
  • Controls: drag to move the map, pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap to select. With soldiers selected, tap the ground to move or an enemy to attack. On a computer: right-click to command, Shift-drag to box-select, WASD to scroll. The game runs at a walking pace; press the speed button for 2× or 3×.
